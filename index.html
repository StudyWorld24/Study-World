    Total OSSSC CRE (I) 2025 Skill Test 20 MCQs + 2 Typing Passages 50 Minutes 50.00 Marks (Minimum Qualifying: 40% = 20.00 / 50.00 Marks)
  2. The clock is controlled by the server countdown timer displayed at the top-right corner of the exam screen. When the timer for a section reaches 00:00, that section is automatically submitted and the exam advances to the next section (or displays the final scorecard after Section 3).
  3. REAL EXAM MODE (Typing Sections 2 & 3): No yellow word highlighting and no green/red error hints are displayed while typing. Text selection, Copy, Cut, Paste, and Right-Click are strictly disabled. Full Backspace and Arrow-key editing are supported. Detailed word-by-word error analysis is displayed only after test submission.
  4. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen during Section 1 (MCQ) shows the status of each question using the five official TCS iON symbols:
    1 You have not visited the question yet.
    2 You have not answered the question.
    3 You have answered the question.
    4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked the question for review (not evaluated).
    5 ✓ The question(s) "Answered and Marked for Review" will be considered for evaluation.
  5. You can click on the > arrow located to the left of the question palette to collapse the sidebar and maximize the question / typing area, and click < to expand it again.
Navigating & Answering a Question (Section 1 — MCQ):
  1. To answer a question, select one option by clicking its radio button, then click Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next question.
  2. Click Mark for Review & Next to save your response (if selected) and mark the question for review before moving to the next question.
  3. Important TCS iON Rule: Clicking directly on a question number in the Question Palette navigates to that question without saving your currently selected option unless you click Save & Next or Mark for Review & Next.
  4. To deselect a chosen option, click the Clear Response button.

OSSSC CRE (I) 2025 Computer Skill Test — Pattern & Evaluation Rules

  1. Section 1 (Computer Skill MCQ — 20 Minutes): Contains 20 practical multiple-choice questions on MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint, MS Access, Windows, and Internet/Security. Each correct answer carries +1 mark with no negative marking.
  2. Sections 2 & 3 (Mock Computer Typing 1 & 2 — 15 Minutes Each): Each section presents a ~2,500-character (~420+ words) plain-text passage in the upper read-only box and an active typing box below. Live sidebar counters show neutral metrics only (Keystrokes, Backspaces, Total Words, Typed Words, Pending Words).
  3. Official 5-Character Word Evaluation Formula:
    • 1 Standard Word = 5 keystrokes/characters.
    • Full Mistake (1.0 error): Wrong/misspelled word, omitted word, or extra inserted word.
    • Half Mistake (0.5 error): Spacing error, capitalization error, or punctuation error.
    • Total Error Words = Full Mistakes + (0.5 × Half Mistakes).
    • Gross WPM = (Gross Keystrokes / 5) / Time Taken in Minutes.
    • Net WPM = ((Gross Keystrokes − (Total Error Words × 5)) / 5) / Time Taken in Minutes (10× penalty preview also available on the scorecard).
    • Accuracy % = ((Total Typed Words − Total Error Words) / Total Typed Words) × 100.
    • Typing Secured Marks (Max 15.00 per Typing Test) = min(15.00, (Net Valid Keystrokes / Total Passage Keystrokes) × 15.00), where Net Valid Keystrokes = max(0, Gross Keystrokes − (Total Error Words × Penalty)).
    • Grand Total & Qualifying Norm: Section 1 (20 Marks) + Typing Test 1 (15 Marks) + Typing Test 2 (15 Marks) = 50.00 Marks. Securing at least 40% (20.00 / 50.00) marks qualifies the Skill Test.
  4. Your performance is automatically stored in your browser under your Unique Candidate ID (OSSSC-2025-0000). You may re-attempt the test anytime to compare Attempt 1, Attempt 2, Attempt 3, and beyond.
Please note all questions and typing passages will appear in English.
